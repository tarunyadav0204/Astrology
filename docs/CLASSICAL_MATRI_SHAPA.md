# Classical Mātṛ-śāpa calculation

AstroRoshni evaluates the thirteen combinations in *Bṛhat Parāśara Horā
Śāstra*, `Purvajanma-śāpa-dyotana`, verses 34–46 (chapter 83 in the
Chaukhamba/Devachandra Jha arrangement).

Primary text and translation:

- <https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_sociology_astrology/par8190.html>
- <https://www.futurestudyonline.com/future_products/articles_documents/ARTL-17.pdf>

## Scope

The repeated result is `suta-kṣaya` or `santati-kṣaya`: loss or absence of
progeny. The calculator therefore does not use “Matru Dosha” as a broad label
for tension with the mother, the mother's health, Moon affliction, fourth-house
pressure, property, home, or emotional wellbeing.

Every verse has a separate rule ID, `BPHS-MS-34` through `BPHS-MS-46`. A rule
matches only when every stated clause matches. The API returns the actual chart
fact for every clause and does not add a Low/Medium/High score.

## Conservative decisions

- Verse 34 retains the stated alternative: Moon is debilitated **or** hemmed
  between malefics; its remaining clauses are still mandatory.
- Verse 37 `pāpāṃśa` is evaluated as a navāṃśa ruled by Sun, Mars or Saturn.
- Generic malefic association means same-sign conjunction. The debated fifth
  and ninth aspects of Rahu and Ketu are not introduced.
- Contextual benefic occupation or aspect of Houses 4 and 5 is reported but
  does not cancel a matched verse because verses 34–46 state no cancellation.

Verses 47–50 state remedies including Setu bathing, Gayatri recitation,
planet-related charity, feeding Brahmins and circumambulation of a peepal tree.
The product reports these as the text's prescriptions, not as guaranteed or
medical outcomes.
